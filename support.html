Support for Forwardcharm Word Search

Forwardcharm Word Search is a word puzzle game: find the hidden words in a grid of letters - a new themed puzzle every level.

Having a problem, or got feedback?

Get in touch: wordsearch@forwardcharm.co.uk

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Swipe across the letters on the wheel, one continuous movement, to spell a word. Lift your finger to enter it. If it's in the crossword grid, it fills in; if it's a real word that isn't in the grid, it's saved as a bonus word instead.

How do I undo a letter mid-swipe?
Swipe back over an earlier letter you've already picked - that backspaces to it, without needing to lift your finger.

What are the Shuffle, Reveal Letter, and Bonus Words buttons?
Shuffle rearranges the letters on the wheel. Reveal Letter fills in one random unsolved square on the grid. Bonus Words shows every extra word you've found so far this level. All three can be used as often as you like.

How do bonus words score?
One point per letter, tallied up on the level-complete screen.

Is there a limit to how many levels there are?
No — every level is generated on the fly from its level number, so there's always another one, and everyone gets the same puzzle for a given level number.

Can I lose?
No. There are no time limits and no way to fail.

Does the app collect any of my data?
No. See the privacy policy for details.
