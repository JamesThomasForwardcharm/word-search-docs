Privacy Policy for Forwardcharm Word Search

Last updated: September 2026

Forwardcharm Word Search ("the app") does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information.

  • No account or sign-in is required to use the app.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.
  • The app does not connect to the internet or send any data to a server.
  • Your level progress and your music and sound effect volume settings are saved locally on your device only, and are never transmitted anywhere.
  • No data is shared with third parties, because no data is collected in the first place.

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if new features are added that require data collection), this page will be updated accordingly.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact: wordsearch@forwardcharm.co.uk

Forwardcharm Word Search is developed by Forwardcharm.
